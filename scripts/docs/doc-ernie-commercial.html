The business. Get Lucky Golf is the fourball's money game in an app — skins, nassau and dots on a live ledger, settled on the 18th — with the one thing no scorecard app can offer on the slip: a Santam-underwritten $100,000 hole-in-one the whole group buys into. Peer games are free forever; the company earns from a $10/month subscription and the insured jackpot — never from the players' bets.
