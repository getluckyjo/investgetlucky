GET LUCKY GOLF × ERNIE ELS

The founding partnership · August 2026 · Confidential

The arrangement in one line. Ernie Els holds a founding 5% stake, structured non-diluting, granted for his name, likeness and network. He invests no cash, draws no fee, and carries no ambassador calendar — the association is the asset, and the equity aligns it with the company's own milestones.

What the partnership is

Name & likenessFounding partner on the platform, the marketing and the prize moment. "Ernie Els is a founding partner" is the sentence that makes a South African golf platform legitimate at global scale.
Credibility that compoundsHis standing opens the doors the plan needs next: underwriters, sponsors, simulator operators and — in time — acquirers. The association de-risks every serious conversation before it starts.
The networkWarm introductions where natural: course and tour relationships, the Els Club properties in Dubai and Malaysia, and the simulator operators on the target list. Opt-in, never an obligation.
Presence, cappedOne to two days a year at most — a shoot, an appearance, a cameo. Entirely his choice which to take up.

Why him

Four majors, 70+ wins, World Golf Hall of Fame — and a track record of judging golf technology early: he was the first official investor in 18Birdies, the round-companion app that proved golfers adopt this category at 10m+ scale. He has seen what a golf app becomes; he chose to build the money-game layer with us.
