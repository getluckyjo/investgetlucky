2 · The assumptions behind it

The productThe Slip — the fourball's bet card (skins, nassau, dots, teams), live ledger, netted settlement. Peer games are free forever: no rake, no per-game fee, no custody. Private bets between players are lawful precisely while no third party takes a fee or holds the pot; every durable competitor (Skins App at $40/yr flat, 18Birdies points-only) reached the same structure. Peer money in play is an engagement KPI carried at zero revenue, permanently.
Subscription$10/month for access to the app — games, ledger, handicaps, season history. Organizer-pays: creating games needs a subscription; invited players ride free for the round. Includes no swings; carries no prize risk.
LaunchUSD-led global, honour mode — players settle among themselves via payment-app deep-links; no wallet, no custody anywhere at launch. Pride Mode (points only) is the default state and the restrictive-market fallback. 2,000 organizers converted from the 60,000 members reached, ramped over six months from zero, from January 2027.
Growth & churn12% → 9.5% → 8% gross monthly (the slip seats three guests per game — the invite loop), churn struck at a hard 5.5%/month, defended by group lock-in. Long-term 7.3% gross from year four.
The game layer2.8 active players per subscriber (2.2 sceptic / 3.4 bull) · 1 money round per active per month · 4 players per slip · ~$40 average peer pot.
The insured jackpotGroup-pooled on the slip: 35% attach (20% sceptic / 55% bull) at a $10 average ticket per player, net 66% after the 24% premium and venue share. Tiers $1 → $500 to $100 → $100,000, filmed and verified. The attach rate is the decisive assumption and is measured by the 90-day pilot on the 25 installed courses before the app ships.
InsuranceThe Santam submission, unchanged: weighted average entry $17.00, premium $4.08, expected claim $1.30 — 31.9% loss ratio, 2.51× breakeven on ace frequency. Premium is ceded on the same swing that creates the risk; the subscription carries zero prize risk. No revised policy is bound as at August 2026.
Cost baseBudgeted line by line. Central is a stated budget — R11m (2027), R28m (2029), R88m (2032). On top: processing 3% of gross collections; verification $0.50 per insured swing only — peer games verify themselves; CAC $24 per gross new organizer (invited seats are free); brand 15% of revenue. Total cost R16.5m → R46.8m → R197.2m.
FundingPeak cumulative drawdown R6.0m in 2027 against the R8m raise; EBITDA positive from 2028, cumulative cash from 2029, no further round assumed. The raise is investment spend — the app build, the pilot, the sign-offs, market entry — not burn.
Entry split (installed)66 / 10 / 24 — Get Lucky / course / Santam insurance. 25 flagship jackpot holes, adding 25 a year, 400 entries per course at the realised R150 average.
Simulators$1 entry / $1,000 insured prize inside operator software, split 46 / 30 / 24. Attach on South Korea's ~94M annual rounds alone: 0.3% → 0.8% → 1.5% (2029) → 4.0% (2032). Machine-measured, so insured closest-to-the-pin works in this channel. No operator agreement signed and no approach made as at August 2026 — Ernie's relationships are the route.
SponsorshipSantam R9m over three years is signed (R2.5m → R3.0m → R3.5m), held flat at the R3.5m run-rate after 2028. New territories at entry: US R2.0m/yr from 2029 rising to R4.0m, Europe R3.0m from 2030, Japan R2.0m from 2031 — partners not yet in place. The slip, the settlement card and the 19th-hole moment are new sponsor inventory.
Gating milestonesP2P counsel opinion (the existing insured-challenge opinion does not cover the peer layer) · Santam written sign-off on the peer-game wrapper · Ernie's counsel sign-off · geo-gating flags from first commit · the 90-day pilot, published.
Revenue multiple3.0× revenue, flat at every forward milestone. No listed comparable exists for an insured skill-prize platform, so the multiple is set against what can be evidenced: R4.0m traded, R9m contracted, a plan costed line by line. The round implies 3.69× (R53.3m ÷ R14.5m 2027 forecast), so the multiple compresses. At R45.3m pre this is roughly 11× trailing revenue — high for South Africa, priced on the contracts and the shareholder in hand.
