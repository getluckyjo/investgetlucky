2 · The assumptions behind it

Launch base2,000 subscribers — the raise-funded launch converts them from the 60,000 members already reached
Subscription$10/month for a $10,000 insured prize per verified ace
Growth & churn10.5% → 8% → 7% gross monthly growth — slowed deliberately each year — with churn held at 4% (net 6.5% / 4% / 3%)
Gross margin66% — insurance premium 24% of revenue, CAC $12
Entry split (installed)66 / 10 / 24 — Get Lucky gross profit / course / Santam insurance, matching the 66% margin
Simulator channel — unit$1 entry for a $1,000 insured prize, built into the operator's software. Split 46 / 30 / 24 — Get Lucky / operator / insurer. The operator takes more than a course's 10% because it brings the venues, the software and the distribution, and Get Lucky installs no hardware.
Simulator channel — volumeAttach rate applied to ~94M annual simulator rounds in South Korea alone: 0.3% (2027) → 0.8% (2028) → 1.5% (2029) → 4.0% (2032). 2026 is an integration year with no revenue. Golfzon's venues outside Korea and every other operator are excluded.
